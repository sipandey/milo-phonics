# Early Years Observation Guide

Date prepared: 16 November 2026

## Purpose

Learn whether children and adults can understand and use the picture listening, replay/continue, and blending interactions. This is formative product research, not an effectiveness study. Do not infer ability from age or treat successful taps as proof of learning.

## Participants and context

- Invite families across the intended early-years range and note each child's relevant communication and access context without treating age as a proxy for readiness.
- Include the adult who usually supports the child. Record device type, setting, language(s) used, familiarity with the app, session length, and interruptions.
- Obtain appropriate caregiver consent and follow the organisation's safeguarding and privacy procedures. Do not record identifying details or audio/video unless separately authorised.
- Stop or take a break whenever the child disengages or the adult asks.

## Session outline

1. Let the child and adult explore Listen & Talk without teaching the controls.
2. Offer the “Listen and find” activity; observe how they replay, choose, and use or decline an adult hint.
3. Offer one Sound Train word; observe sound taps, pause for an attempt, replay, and continuation.
4. Ask the adult what felt clear, confusing, rushed, or useful. Avoid asking the child to perform or repeat a sound on demand.

## Observation prompts

| Topic | Record observable behaviour and context |
| --- | --- |
| Understanding actions | What did the child try after seeing each screen? Which control, if any, needed explanation? |
| Replay and pacing | Did the child replay, wait, move on, or leave? Was the pause long enough? |
| Picture recognition | What did the child point to or call each picture? Note ambiguity without correcting them. |
| Engagement | What held or lost attention? Include interruptions and non-screen interests. |
| Adult involvement | What did the adult say or do? Did the prompts invite conversation without pressure? |
| Access and rendering | Note device, emoji appearance, reachability, audio audibility, and any motor/sensory barriers. |
| Hints and attempts | Record whether a hint was used and describe the interaction; do not score hinted responses as independent. |
