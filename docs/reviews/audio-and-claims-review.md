# Audio and Educational Claims Review

Date: 16 November 2026

## Current status

- The app routes phoneme sounds through local audio assets and word/sentence audio through configured local or generated CDN assets. The generated manifest identifies generated recordings; this metadata does not establish accent accuracy or phonics suitability.
- Automated file/mapping checks do not establish what a clip sounds like. A general speech recogniser is not reliable enough to approve isolated phonemes.
- No suitably experienced phonics specialist has signed off the complete phoneme set or representative blends. Do not claim specialist approval, Oxford authenticity, accreditation, or proven learning outcomes until evidence is recorded.
- The Parent Dashboard now states that audio review is pending and describes voice qualities as unverified.
